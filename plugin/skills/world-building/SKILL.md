# Building the World

The World is the DM's truth: Entities (NPCs, Places, Factions, Topics) with Pages and Facts. The Players see only what their Characters come to believe.

1. **Start from the Party.** For each Character, pick one or two hooks from their backstory, bond or flaw: a person they know, a place they come from, a debt, a rival. The World should have room for every Character.
2. **Find the spine.** From the Seed's premise and the hooks, decide what is wrong in the world and who profits from it. That tension feeds the Arc.
3. **Establish the opening ground.** One starting Place and the Place the first Act heads for, linked with `[[Name]]` links in their Pages.
4. **Establish the key cast.** A patron or ally, an antagonist and one or two people tied to a Character's hook. Each NPC gets a want, a voice and a secret if the story needs one. Write the secret as a Fact; the Players learn it only in play.
5. **Seed what NPCs know** with `establish`'s `knows`: what each one knows, believes wrongly or is hiding. Knowledge is data, never a note in a Page.
6. **One faction at most** at the start, unless the Seed's scale calls for more.
7. **Check your work.** `search` before establishing anything that might exist already; `establish` refuses near-duplicates, so name them in `distinct_from` only when they really are different.

Keep Pages short: what the DM needs to play the Entity. Facts are single, checkable statements. Leave the rest for play to discover.
